"The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog." is an English-language pangram-a sentence that contains all the letters of the English alphabet. Owing to its brevity and coherence, it has become widely known. The phrase is commonly used for touch-typing practice, testing typewriters and computer keyboards, displaying examples of fonts, and other applications involving text where the use of all letters in the aplhabet is desired.
