UNIVERSITY OF ST. LASALLE

VISION & MISSION

VISION

University of St. La Salle is a premier, responsive learning community,
advancing human and Christian education, through research, innovation, and
social engagement in Asia and the Pacific, in the service of humanity.

MISSION

True to our Catholic tradition and inspired by St. John Baptist de La Salle,
University of St. La Salle forms persons of integrity and excellence, committed
to the shared mission of caring for peoples and the Earth.
